Supply Agreement No. 21-E
Tashkent, 2 November 2026
Gamma Trade LLC (the Supplier) and the Buyer named in the Order (the Buyer) agree as follows.
1. SUBJECT
1.1. The Supplier shall supply and deliver the Goods listed in each Order, and the Buyer shall accept and pay for them.
1.2. Each Order states the Goods, their quantity, price and the date of delivery.
2. DELIVERY
2.1. The Goods are delivered DDP the Buyer's warehouse (Incoterms 2020).
2.2. Title and risk in the Goods pass to the Buyer on delivery to the carrier.
3. PRICE AND PAYMENT
3.1. The Buyer shall pay each invoice within ninety (90) days of the invoice date.
3.2. The Buyer shall pay a late payment interest of 0.1% of the overdue amount per day.
3.3. All payments are made in US dollars to the account stated in the invoice.
3.4. The Buyer may set off any amount the Supplier owes it against any payment due to the Supplier.
4. ACCEPTANCE
4.1. The Buyer shall inspect the Goods on delivery and notify the Supplier of any defects within sixty (60) days.
5. WARRANTY
5.1. The Supplier warrants that the Goods are free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months from installation.
5.2. The warranty does not cover wear and tear or damage caused by improper use.
6. LIABILITY
6.1. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses, including loss of profit.
7. TERM AND TERMINATION
7.1. This Agreement remains in force until 31 December 2027.
7.2. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice.
8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
8.1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
8.2. Disputes are finally settled by arbitration at the London Court of International Arbitration.
